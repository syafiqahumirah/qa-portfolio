The human behind the bugs.

Curious by nature, detail oriented by profession. I enjoy untangling complicated problems and asking the questions everyone else might have skipped.

My Background −

Different industries. Different challenges.

I've tested banking journeys, insurance workflows and healthcare systems. Each came with its own rules, stakeholders and surprises. Moving between teams has taught me to learn quickly, ask for context and adapt my approach instead of relying on one playbook.

How I Work

The habits I bring to a project, especially when the answer isn't obvious.

01 / LOOK WIDER

Beyond the Ticket

I check what a change might affect elsewhere, from connected features to the people using them. A passed acceptance criterion isn't always the whole story.

02 / USE JUDGMENT

When Time Is Tight

I prioritize business critical flows, make testing gaps visible and give stakeholders the context they need to make release decisions.

I've helped make automation steps more reusable and testing approaches more consistent, so the next person doesn't have to start from scratch.
