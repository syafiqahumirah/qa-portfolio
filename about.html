How I Approach Quality

Testing matters. But knowing what to test, why it matters and how to move a team forward matters too.

01 / THINK AHEAD

Ask Better Questions

I look beyond the happy path, challenge unclear requirements and think about risks before they become production problems.

02 / TAKE OWNERSHIP

Own the Outcome

I prioritize critical scenarios, communicate testing risks and make sure quality conversations happen before release decisions.

03 / IMPROVE THE PROCESS

Make Things Better

I look for opportunities to standardize testing approaches, improve reusable automation and help teams work more consistently.
